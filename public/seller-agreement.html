KuraliUpdates Seller Agreement

Version seller-terms-2026-11-01 · Draft for legal review before public launch

Important: This draft is an operational agreement and must be reviewed by Indian marketplace counsel before production activation. The contracting entity must be confirmed as the correct incorporated entity before publication.

1. Seller eligibility and information

You confirm that you are legally entitled to operate the business and that the details, shop address, contact information, GSTIN, FSSAI details and documents submitted are complete and accurate. You must promptly update expired, changed or withdrawn licences.

2. Licences and legal compliance

You are responsible for obtaining and maintaining registrations and licences applicable to your business and products, including GST registration where required, FSSAI registration/licence for applicable food businesses, local trade permissions and product-specific approvals. Submission of a number or certificate does not mean KuraliUpdates or a government authority has verified it.

3. Product listings and consumer protection

You must list genuine, lawful products with accurate descriptions, prices, quantities, taxes, availability, expiry information and applicable warnings. You must not list prohibited, counterfeit, unsafe, expired or misleading products. You must honour accepted orders and applicable consumer-protection obligations.

4. Orders, fulfilment and customer service

You are responsible for accurate order acceptance, safe packing, product quality, timely handover, invoices where required, and cooperation with delivery, complaints, returns and refunds. Do not contact customers for unrelated marketing or misuse their personal information.

5. Refunds, cancellations and payment reconciliation

Refunds and cancellations will be processed under the KuraliUpdates customer refund policy and applicable law. You must cooperate with investigations and reimburse amounts properly attributable to seller error, defective/non-conforming goods, misdescription or non-fulfilment, subject to the published seller settlement terms. No minimum seller refund reserve is created by this agreement.

6. Fees, settlement and records

Applicable commissions, delivery charges, taxes, settlement timing and deductions will be disclosed separately before activation or in the seller dashboard. You must maintain accurate business and transaction records and provide lawful supporting information when requested.

7. Seller documents and verification

Documents are collected for onboarding and compliance review. Access is restricted to authorised personnel. Verification status may be pending, verified or rejected; a file upload is not government verification. You authorise reasonable checks with relevant issuing authorities or authorised verification services where legally permitted.

8. Privacy and security

Personal and business information will be processed under the KuraliUpdates privacy notice and applicable data-protection law. Do not upload Aadhaar, bank credentials or unrelated sensitive identity information unless specifically requested through a lawful, secure process. You must protect customer data and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

9. Suspension and termination

KuraliUpdates may withhold activation, suspend listings or suspend/terminate access for false information, expired or missing mandatory licences, unsafe or prohibited products, fraud, repeated service failures, legal directions or material breach. Where appropriate, reasons and an opportunity to respond will be provided.

10. Intellectual property and content

You grant KuraliUpdates a limited, non-exclusive licence to display your approved shop name, logo, product images and listing content solely to operate and promote the marketplace. You confirm that you have rights to the content you provide.

11. Changes, disputes and governing law

Material changes will be communicated through the platform. This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes, jurisdiction and any arbitration mechanism must be finalised by counsel and inserted before launch.

12. Acceptance

By checking the acceptance box and submitting your application, you confirm that you have read and agree to this version of the Seller Agreement, subject to final publication by the confirmed contracting entity.
